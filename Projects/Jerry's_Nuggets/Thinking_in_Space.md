# Thinking in Space
 (draft) 
*Subhed.* 

Video 2 — The Brain tour:

The live dispatch. You promised to take them across; now you do. This is the episode that turns the abstract boundary into something they can watch you stand on.

**The promise:** show what it actually changes to have thirty years of thinking externalized, connected, and accountable — every idea linked, every link a decision. This is Brain-as-thinking-instrument, not Brain-as-storage (the Tversky territory: reasoning you do by moving through space).

**The spine:** open at your home node. Navigate one serious topic for about ninety seconds, then one genuinely surprising one for another ninety — the familiar/unexpected pairing that shows range without sprawl. Narrate the *choices*, not the software: why this link and not that one, what a branch remembers that you'd forgotten. Resist the urge to branch into every adjacent thread; one clean path beats a tangle.

**Visual:** screen recording of the plex in motion, your voice over it. This is the first episode that needs footage — which is another reason to record it early.

**Trailing hook:** close by pointing forward — you've shown them *what* an externalized mind does; next week, *why* it matters for the version of this they're living, the moment they wonder whether the machine is replacing them.
