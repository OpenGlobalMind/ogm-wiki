# The Shelf

How the clusters on [[Threads]] roll up into books. Drafted 11 Nov 2026 with Claude from Jerry's decisions that evening. Working names throughout; titles are Jerry's.

## Five levels

1. **Nugget**: one idea, freestanding.
2. **Post**: a page in Jerry's voice that pulls in two or three nuggets.
3. **Cluster (chapter)**: a run of posts. These are the sections on [[Threads]].
4. **Book**: a red thread laid over the landscape of nuggets. One ordering among many.
5. **Community**: the people thinking together around the nuggets. Collective hybrid intelligence.

Describing these levels is itself a post. It belongs in the Books and NeoBooks cluster, which may be renamed (Jerry floated "Messing with Media").

## Open questions

- Where do these clusters land: Maps as boundary objects, The future of work has a trust problem, Books and NeoBooks?
- Clusters not yet on a book: GenAI and capitalism, What I saw early, Seer and advisor, Next stacks and a better future, Politics and democracy.
- The trust book has competing outlines. Title Jerry loves: What If We Trusted You? (also his TEDx title; he owns the domains). A more direct, didactic Design from Trust version felt too boring. Whether the contrarians and "how I got here" sit in the middle of this book is undecided.
- Genre: Jerry wants these books rooted in human story and visibly playful, not a recitation of arguments, and is open to mixing genres. He is not sure he is a novelist.
- Shared opening for every book: the "thank you for buying this souvenir" paragraph as the one nugget all the books have in common. Held for later.
- A further cluster: Design from Trust as a way to rethink every sector (education, elections, science into policy). Close to the Rethinking beat and the thread Next stacks and a better future.
- "NeoBooks" is a name chosen because books are valued; the point is the community and the nuggets living in it.
